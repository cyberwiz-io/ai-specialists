The files in `.claude-plugin/` and `plugins/` are generated by the build from the cards and the coordinators; never edit them by hand. To change one, edit its card in `catalog/` or the generator in `scripts/packlib/plugin.py` or `scripts/packlib/adapter.py`.

1. `python3 -B scripts/pack.py build`, then `python3 -B scripts/pack.py check`.
2. `python3 -B -m unittest discover -s tests -p "test_plugins.py"`.
3. `claude plugin validate .` when the `claude` CLI is available, and report its output as is.
4. `git status --short plugins/ .claude-plugin/` and summarize what changed and why (card edit, coordinator edit, or generator change).

Report that the plugins are structurally checked and have no recorded live-host evidence unless a trial has been added. Do not commit.
