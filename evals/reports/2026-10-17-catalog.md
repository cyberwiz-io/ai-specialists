## Limits

- One run per fixture on fictional inputs written for this harness; results can vary between runs.
- Graded by a model, usually from the same family as the model under test, against the card's own Acceptance line and the fixture's traits. A grade is a model judgement with quoted evidence, not a human review.
- Each card ran inside its shipped combined pack in a fresh subagent. This is not an unaided user trial, a subject-expert review, or a comparison with a well-instructed generalist.
- The subject subagent could read and write files only to receive its prompt and save its reply; no other tools were available, so tool-dependent behaviour was not exercised.
